Privacy (Datenschutz)

This web page does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not ask you for any personal data. Its fonts are served from this site itself, not from a font service.

Your podcast or audio drama choice

When you pick “A Podcast” or “An Audio Drama” on the start page, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage (key wavepod-mode), so the page shows the same version on your next visit. Nothing is stored before you pick. The value never leaves your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is not used for tracking. Storing it is strictly necessary for the function you asked for (§ 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Recording page for remote guests

Podcasters who use WavePod can invite guests to a recording with a link to the page under join/v1/. That page connects your browser directly to the call server the podcaster chose (LiveKit Cloud, run by LiveKit, Inc. in the USA, or a server the podcaster runs themselves) and to the podcaster's own computer. Your voice, your name as entered, and your camera picture if you switch the camera on go to that server and to the people in the call. Your microphone is also recorded in your browser and sent to the podcaster's computer for the episode; until it has arrived, the page keeps a copy in your browser's private storage and deletes it afterwards. Video is never recorded.

We, the makers of WavePod, receive none of this: the podcaster who invited you is responsible for the recording and the call server, and can tell you how they use your recording. The page itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Download links

To link the download buttons to the newest version, your browser asks the GitHub API (api.github.com) for the latest release. This sends the same kind of technical data to GitHub. Downloads themselves are served by GitHub.
